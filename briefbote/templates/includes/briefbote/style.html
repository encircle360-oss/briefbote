{#- The one stylesheet of the print formats of Briefbote: A4 portrait, the text 25 mm from the left and 20 mm from the right edge (DIN 5008), the font Inter embedded from the app, black on white with grey rules. Classes of their own under .bb, since Frappe's print stylesheet styles bare elements and classes. Both PDF generators of Frappe read the page margins from the rule on .print-format alone; wkhtmltopdf ignores @page, page-break-after and flexbox, so the sheet keeps to blocks, tables and absolute positions, and keeps rows and blocks together with page-break-inside: avoid. It does not rely on Frappe's print bundle. A print format sets letter (briefbote_letter) before it includes this sheet; its content starts with first_page.html and ends with footer.html. -#}
